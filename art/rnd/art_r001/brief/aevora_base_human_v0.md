# AEVORA Base Human v0 — original text brief

Registry ID ARTCHAR-R001; slug aevora_base_human_v0; status rnd_candidate. A pipeline-test human adventurer / settlement resident, not a final protagonist or lore/faction decision.

Create one original full-body human in an AEVORA 3/4 top-down hybrid view: square-grid gameplay, dimensional presentation, pixel/semi-pixel richness, **not diamond isometric**. Readable head, torso, arms and legs; stable proportions and identity across down/left/right/up. Neutral practical clothing: muted teal simple tunic, warm gray trousers, plain brown boots, short dark chestnut hair. No weapon, backpack, text or existing/copyrighted-character resemblance. Clothing colors are this experiment's identity cues, not a canonical palette.

Prefer silhouette and clean hard pixels over extra detail. Transparent background with hard alpha, no painted ground/shadow, no antialiasing or blurry resampling. Every output frame keeps a complete 64×64 canvas. Ground/root comparison target (32,44), leaving top/side/bottom movement margin; feet plausibly meet that ground line. Never independently trim/recenter/rescale frames. Frame canvas, root, camera and character scale remain fixed.

First generate only one down-facing neutral master, then review at native 1× and nearest 2×. Check anatomy, gameplay readability, original identity, safe canvas fit, transparent motion margins and plausible feet/root before approving an anchor or creating animation. Idle targets 4 frames/direction; Walk 6 frames/direction, 150ms looping. No Run, attack, equipment, hurt/death or NPC generator.

Craftpix is only an in-engine scale/cadence benchmark. Do not attach vendor PNGs, imitate its style, or describe this candidate as copied from Craftpix. Approved candidate masters may later be identity references for the same original character.
